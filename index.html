I want to go to university because I want to learn more, discover new things, and grow into a more independent person. I want to gain knowledge and experiences that can help me understand what I’m capable of and what I want to achieve in the future.

Most importantly, I want to build a future where I can keep learning, create something meaningful, and eventually explore the world while doing something I love.
